Music & Arts

Spartan Fall Fling

Saturday, October 3 - 4:00-9:00 PM
College Avenue

The Campus Activities Board closes out the afternoon with student bands, a DJ set, food trucks, and lawn games along College Avenue.

About the Event

Spartan Fall Fling is organized by the Campus Activities Board (CAB). Doors open at 4:00 PM, with the first act starting at 4:30 PM on the College Avenue stage.

This year's lineup mixes student performers with a local DJ set, plus a t-shirt giveaway and lawn games between sets.

What to Expect

College Avenue fills up quickly after 5:00 PM, so bring a blanket or chair if you want to sit near the stage.

  • Bag check at both entrances
  • Outside food is welcome, but no glass containers or alcohol
  • Free earplugs are available at the sound booth

Schedule

  • 4:00 PM - Gates open, food trucks begin service
  • 4:30 PM - Student open-mic set
  • 5:30 PM - Local student band
  • 6:30 PM - Spartan step team performance
  • 7:15 PM - Second student band
  • 8:00 PM - Closing DJ set

Accessibility & Participation

A reserved, level viewing area is available near the sound booth. The event is free and open to everyone, no ticket needed, and Spiro will be around for photos throughout the evening.

Event Details

  • Date: Saturday, October 3, 2026
  • Time: 4:00 PM - 9:00 PM
  • Location: College Avenue
  • Organizer: Campus Activities Board
  • Admission: Free and open to the public
